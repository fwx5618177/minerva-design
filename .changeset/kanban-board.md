KanbanBoard / KanbanColumn on every renderer: a horizontally scrolling board of equal-width status columns (columnWidth, fill, gap, height / maxHeight with vertically scrolling column bodies, scroll snapping on narrow screens) and columns with an accent color, a title, a count badge announced with the title, a description, actions, a role=list of cards, translated empty content and a footer. Drag and drop is not built in: move cards with your own actions.
